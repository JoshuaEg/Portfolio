**App Description:** 
Tomodachi Test is a game where you take care of fake children/pets/or somthing by simply remembering to feed them everyday.
In order to do this you must simply remember the name of every child you create and click a button. If you dont remember to
feed those who you are responsible for, it will die, and you will feel very bad about it. Your stats are displayed in your login
page telling you days since your last death, your alive to total ratio, and a judgement on your character.

Lore if you care: Tomodachi Test is a study conducted by some extraterestial advanced life form that thinks humans are contradictory
mess of a species, how ever all of his intellectual peers beleive the strong human spirit is nothing to underestimate. To prove them
wrong he set up a simple game (this) to see if humans are capable of doing the simplest of tasks.
